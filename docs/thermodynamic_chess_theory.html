Thermodynamic Chess

A chess engine as an annealing computation: emergent temperature, Boltzmann-counting optionality, the phase structure of positions — and the search tree and the position as one thermodynamics
Conceptual summary of the fablethermochess engine, fourth edition (the annealed model, with the search–thermodynamics correspondence and the non-equilibrium reading of play)  ·  July 2026
Abstract. Conventional engines value a chess position by minimax. This engine replaces that rule with statistical mechanics, and removes every prescribed thermodynamic quantity from the model. Energy is geometric material alone. Entropy is optionality: at the search horizon a side's move ensemble is uniform, so its entropy is Boltzmann's own S = ln W with W the number of available moves. Temperature is not chosen but measured: the search is an explicit annealing process in which iterative deepening is time, and the bath temperature is the ensemble-weighted residual fluctuation of the engine's own value revisions — cooling toward the T = 0 minimax ground state as the search converges, floored only by the zero-point quantum of the evaluation lattice. Position value is the free energy F = ⟨Q⟩ + T·S at the bath temperature, so activity trades against material only at the rate the engine's genuine uncertainty licenses. Prior editions established this model and its instrument certifications: the thermometer is bitwise-deterministic and enumeration-invariant; at a tablebase-certified mutual zugzwang the tempo's chemical potential lands on its gauge zero; and the initiative's Curie–Weiss self-coupling is real, attractive, and opponent-dependent, with adversarial chess played at the initiative's Curie point within measurement error. The fourth edition adds the piece the model had been missing: a closed-form correspondence between the search-tree variables — depth, node count, thinking time — and the position's thermodynamic variables. The effective branching factor of the tree is the exponential of the ensemble entropy, so the log node budget partitions as ln N = S̄·d + ln Qℓ — a conservation law in which depth and width are conjugate through a fixed resource, and in which the same entropy S is metered once by temperature into the choice premium T·S (a value) and once by depth into the branching cost S·d (a cost). This single identity organizes the whole experimental program: every attempt to repartition value between width and depth is one dial, and it has failed six ladders deep, because the premium T·S is simultaneously the model's optionality value and its optimizer's curse — the two are the same term (Eε[max] − ⟨Q⟩ = T(S+γ), the Gumbel identity). Measured along a game, F is not a martingale: it bleeds a persistent negative current under best play, so the position is an equilibrium object but the game is a non-equilibrium steady state, and the winner's-curse bleed is its entropy production. In this frame the opponent is a second reservoir at a different temperature — the two-temperature generalized Gibbs ensemble that the grand potential and the Gibbs-leaf paradigm were already approximating — and the strength ceiling is a statement about which affinity the engine's attention is allocated against. The lawful frontier the correspondence exposes is a controller that banks thinking time against the measured local dissipation (a fluctuation–dissipation estimate of the horizon deficit) rather than against value noise, because that is the one channel that reads off-horizon danger without paying the depth to see it.

1. The central idea

A chess position with the player to move is treated as a physical system. Its accessible microstates are its legal moves; each move a has a value Qa — the negated energy of the microstate (Q = −E: good moves are low-energy states) — obtained recursively from the position it leads to. The system occupies its microstates with canonical probabilities and is valued by its free energy:

πa = eQa/T / Z ,  F = T ln Z = ⟨Q⟩ + T·S ,  S = −Σa πa ln πa (1)

Maximizing F is minimizing E − TS. The decomposition is the heart of the design: a position is valuable for the quality of its best continuations and for the entropy of having many comparably good ones — but the entropy is paid at the going temperature, and everything hinges on what sets T. The premium Π ≡ T·S = F − ⟨Q⟩ is the object the whole program turns on; §11 shows it wears two faces — optionality value and selection bias — that are provably the same number.

Search proceeds in negamax form, Qa = −F(position after a), with the identical rule for both sides. As T → 0 the log-sum-exp collapses to the maximum and the engine is exact minimax; the engine always plays argmaxa Qa, with the weights πa displayed as a measurement of how contested the choice was. Equation (1) is the soft Bellman backup of maximum-entropy reinforcement learning, and two-sided Boltzmann best response is a logit quantal-response equilibrium; what distinguishes this engine is that T is an emergent, measured quantity (§4), and — new in this edition — that the same ensemble that sets the value also sets the geometry of the search that computes it (§5).

2. Energy: geometric material, and nothing else

Nothing is imported from chess folklore — no 1/3/3/5/9 table, no piece-square bonuses. A piece type's value is its mean x-ray reach: the number of squares it sweeps from a square on an empty board, averaged over the 64 squares. The pawn's base is 2 (its typical move count; also the display unit: 1♙ = 2 internal units) and the king's is 0 (never traded; both sides always have exactly one, so any base cancels in the difference). The static energy of a position is the material difference of these bases — only that:

What was removed, and why. The first edition added each piece's instantaneous blocker-aware mobility to the energy at full weight, making one square of reach worth half a pawn — a hidden hardcoded exchange rate between material and activity, roughly ten times larger than any calibrated engine uses. Match diagnosis against a pure-material oracle showed exactly the failure this predicts: moves that lost a knight and moves that kept material backed up with identical values, because routine mobility swings are the same magnitude as a minor piece's base. Mobility is real value, but it is not energy; it is optionality, and optionality is entropy (§3) — priced at temperature, as physics demands. When the engine's uncertainty is low, activity is nearly worthless against material; exactly as it should be.

3. Entropy: optionality as Boltzmann counting

Interior nodes get optionality for free — it is the T·S term of (1), propagated by the backup. Leaves need a static term, and here the model makes its cleanest statement. At the search horizon the engine has no energy resolution among the unsearched continuations: the correct ensemble over a side's moves is therefore uniform — maximum entropy — and its entropy is Boltzmann's counting formula S = ln W, with W the side's bare (pseudo-legal) move count. The leaf evaluation is

U = material + T·(ln Wus − ln Wthem) (2)

— side-symmetric by construction (no parity bias between odd and even search depths), bounded, logarithmic (the tenth extra move is worth far less than the second), and computable in a single board scan. A position in check contributes material only: passing is illegal there, the ensemble is not stationary, and a non-equilibrium state has no entropy to sell. Trapped pieces, open files, and centralization all still register — as counting, at temperature — but a piece of material can never be outbid by activity unless the bath is hot enough to license it, and the bath is only hot when the engine genuinely cannot tell its best moves apart (§4).

Two entropies, one shape. It pays to keep the leaf term ln W (a maximum-entropy prior over the unseen, side-symmetric, standing in for horizon ignorance) distinct from the interior premium T·S (the softmax choice premium of (1), propagated by the backup). Both are T·S-shaped, but they answer different objections and behave differently under deepening: the leaf term is the horizon correction and dissolves as the horizon recedes; the interior premium is the accumulated optimizer's curse of the soft backup and — measured — does not fully dissolve (§11). Most of the experimental program is about the interior premium; the leaf term is the one horizon correction the model gets for free precisely because counting both sides is free (contrast §10's initiative, where measuring both sides is not).

4. Temperature: the annealing residual

There is no prescribed heat capacity, no material schedule, no slider. The search is an explicit annealing computation: iterative deepening is time, deepening is cooling, and the bath temperature is measured. Whenever the search revises a root value by looking deeper, the revision |ΔQ| is one sample of the engine's residual uncertainty about its own evaluations. The bath temperature for the next iteration is the σ-scale of that fluctuation ensemble,

T ← √(Tmeas² + T₀²) ,  Tmeas = 1.2533 · ⟨ |Q(d) − Q(d−2)| ⟩π (3)

with three qualifications, each learned from a failure mode observed in play:

  1. Ensemble-weighted. The average in (3) is Boltzmann-weighted by the current occupancies π. The revision of a move the distribution does not occupy is resolved knowledge, not residual fluctuation — an unweighted thermometer reads every refuted blunder as heat and boils.
  2. Same-parity. Negamax-softmax values carry an alternating entropy ladder — every node adds +T·S for its mover — so values at adjacent depths differ by one uncompensated layer ~T·S̄. A thermometer comparing depth d to d−1 reads the ladder itself as fluctuation and feeds back T ∝ T: exponential runaway heating (observed: T = 1.4 → 3.4 → 10.9 → 59.7 within one game). Samples therefore compare depth d with d−2 — same leaf parity, ladder cancelled identically. Depth 1 is compared against the static material baseline, so the first reading is the tactical volatility that quiescence uncovered; depth 2 has no clean partner and takes no reading.
  3. Zero-point floor. The evaluation spectrum is quantized — reach comes in whole squares — and a quantized system's fluctuation cannot fall below its level spacing. T₀ is the minimal positive level spacing of the mobility spectrum, computed from the geometry tables (it evaluates to one square). This is the zero-point motion of the evaluation lattice: a converged search cannot distinguish values finer than one square of reach, so optionality retains exactly that much purchase and no more. Without the floor, quiet positions freeze completely and the engine drifts planlessly into repetition; with it, activity is always worth something — about half a pawn of purchase at most.

T is held constant within one deepening iteration — one bath, one temperature, and the transposition table stays coherent — and updated (with damping) between iterations. Forced lines converge and freeze toward minimax; unresolved middlegames run warm; longer thinking time means a colder engine. Perfect play is the fully annealed T → 0 ground state. The measured T is global for the whole search tree: a genuinely canonical ensemble, in contrast to the first edition's per-node temperatures, which scaled with each node's own value spread and thus made the engine most reckless exactly where the stakes were highest.

4.1 Is T well-defined? The invariance battery

The standing objection: T "depends on move order, time limits, depth — accidents of the search, not the position." The defensible claim is narrower and tested (tests/invariance.js). T is a property of the pair (position, protocol) — like every real temperature, which is likewise a property of system plus measurement contact — and at fixed protocol it must be a reproducible function of the position alone. Measured, on fresh baths: determinism (two independent instances at fixed depth return bitwise-identical T, F, S, and best move); gauge invariance under enumeration order (eight seeded permutations of the root move list shift T by at most 4·10−16 — float epsilon, not physics); and explicit protocol dependence (T(budget) is tabulated, not hidden — the reading changes with thinking time exactly where the reached depth changes, tracking depth parity as the ladder analysis predicts). The honest statement: T is not a function on FEN strings and never claimed to be — it is a reproducible, enumeration-invariant observable of a position under a declared annealing schedule, which is precisely the status of temperature in any laboratory. §5 now makes the "protocol dependence" quantitative: the reached depth is a closed-form function of the position's own entropy.

A limitation, recorded rather than hidden: thermal runaway in decided positions. The leaf optionality term T·Δln W scales with T, so a hot bath licenses enormous value swings, which the thermometer then reads back as heat — a positive feedback the same-parity repair bounds but does not eliminate. In roughly 5% of self-play moves (and in games where the engine is being crushed) the bath runs away to T ~ 10²–10⁴ while the game is already decided; the healthy bulk sits at T ≈ 1–3 at every thinking-time rung. Runaway readings carry no usable structure and are excluded from equation-of-state fits by a declared robust cut (T > 50, counts reported). Root allocation (§5, §12) nearly halves the runaway share in play by quenching decided positions deeper; a full cure — saturating the optionality term's thermometer back-reaction — remains open.

5. The search–thermodynamics correspondence

The prior editions treated depth, node count, and thinking time as "accidents of the search" — real, declared as protocol, but outside the physics. They are not outside it. The truncation rule of §7 makes the branching factor of the tree a function of the ensemble, and that one fact closes the loop: the search geometry and the position's thermodynamics are the same variables.

At an interior node the engine fully recurses only the moves the bath keeps live — those within 3T of the best in value, capped at Wmax = 16 (§7). That live set is the hard-cutoff realization of the softmax's own effective support: its size is the ensemble perplexity, capped,

beff = min( eS, Wmax ) (S1)

— cold nodes (small S) narrow toward best-line probing; hot nodes (large S) keep many branches, up to the cap. Because the thermal backup needs the whole sum Z = Σ eQa/T over the live set, there is no alpha–beta √-pruning inside it (cutoffs are unsound when every child contributes to Z, §7); the live set is recursed in full. A search to depth d with a per-leaf quiescence subtree of size Qℓ therefore visits

ln N = S̄·d + ln Qℓ ,  t = τ₀·N (S2)

nodes in wall-time t, where S̄ is the path-mean live-set entropy (bounded by ln Wmax) and τ₀ the mean per-node cost. Inverting at fixed budget gives the reached depth in closed form:

d = ( ln(t/τ₀) − ln Qℓ ) / S̄ (S3)

measured, consistent. This is not a scaling analogy but an equation that reproduces the instrument's own node counts: with beff ≈ 8–10 and Qℓ ≈ 10²–10³, depth 4 predicts ~10⁶ nodes, matching the recorded 5.0M at fixed d4; root allocation, which truncates the root to its dominant set exactly as (S1) truncates an interior node, cut that to 690k — a ~7× drop in root branching, as (S2) requires.

5.1 One conserved resource, two conjugate spends

Read (S2) as a conservation law. The time control fixes t, hence fixes the log node budget ln N = ln(t/τ₀) — the conserved extensive quantity of a move. It partitions into width×depth plus the forcing (quiescence) inflation:

ln N = S̄·d + ln Qℓ = const  (fixed by the clock). (S4)

Depth d and width S̄ are conjugate through this budget, as a variable and its price under a fixed action: the marginal cost of a ply is ∂(ln N)/∂d = S̄, and the marginal cost of keeping one more move live at fixed depth is ∂(ln N)/∂S̄ = d. This is the width-versus-depth tension the whole program keeps colliding with, now stated as a constraint rather than a slogan — and it identifies exactly what is being fought over: not depth, not width, but the partition of the conserved ln N between them.

The hinge: the entropy is metered twice. The same S appears in two places. As a value it is the choice premium Π = T·S of (1). As a cost it is the branching S̄·d of (S4). You cannot price a move's optionality without instantiating it as a live branch — considering the having-of-options is paying the node budget to look at them. This single identity is why the whole "move value between width and depth" program is one dial: premium deflation (backup = mean/basin/sigma) shrinks S and so simultaneously deflates the premium and buys depth via (S3) — and it fails, because the premium was load-bearing (§11); truncation is exactly the device that caps the cost side (beff ≤ Wmax) while leaving the value side (T·S at the root) uncapped, so a cold search pays less on both at once and a hot one keeps its options at bounded expense.

5.2 The depth tax of a forcing extension

Any pre-thermal relaxation that resolves more forcing structure — quiescence extended to include non-capture checks (qCheck, §12) — enlarges the per-leaf subtree Qℓ → ρ·Qℓ. By (S3), at fixed budget this costs depth:

Δd = − ln ρ / S̄ (S5)

measured, predictive. The tax rides the quiescence inflation ρ (how many forcing nodes are actually searched), not the per-node arithmetic cost τ₀. This is why a "fast pre-filter" that cut qCheck's per-node cost twelvefold recovered almost no depth (d 1.67 → 1.92, ~¼ ply): it left ρ unchanged. The correspondence turns what looked like an anomaly into a prediction — and it states the composition law the scheduler experiment (§12) ran into: a depth-costly instrument cannot be married to a depth-conditional one at a fixed per-move budget, because both draw on the same ln N and one of them subtracts ln Qℓ from every move before the other can reallocate what remains.

6. The phase structure, kept as pure measurement

The first edition's central discovery survives with its arbitrariness removed. Every position has an intrinsic heat-capacity curve — the Schottky anomaly of its move ensemble:

C(T) = Varπ(Q) / T² ,  C* = maxT C(T) at T = T̂ (4)

C(T) vanishes at both ends (the distribution freezes onto the best move as T → 0; σ saturates as T → ∞) and peaks between. C* — the largest capacity the position's ensemble can sustain — is a parameter-free invariant, and T̂ its spinodal. Previously C was demanded and T solved for, forcing a choice of branch and a pinning rule; now the bath temperature arrives from the annealing measurement and the dashboard reports where it sits on the curve: frozen (forced positions), cold (ordered side, T < T̂), critical (T ≈ T̂), or hot (disordered side). The peak search uses a robust median scale of the bulk of the Q distribution, so a mate outlier reads as frozen rather than defining a fake high-temperature branch. Note that beff of (S1) and C(T) of (4) are two readings of the same spread: the phase that names where the bath sits also fixes how wide the tree is.

7. Absorbing states: mate and repetition

Checkmate is a ground state, not an energy level. No entropy may attach to a terminal outcome: whenever the best move is a forced win (or every move a forced loss) the backup is pure max Q, with no T ln Z term — otherwise "many ways to mate" would outscore the mate itself and the engine would shuffle checks forever (an observed pathology, now a regression test). Mate scores carry distance: each negation ticks them one unit toward zero, so mate-in-2 strictly outranks mate-in-4 and the scores stay node-relative, keeping transposition entries valid across paths. Mate-scored moves are also excluded from the thermometer ensemble (3): absorbing states carry distance ticks, not thermal fluctuation.

Repetition is an absorbing draw, F = 0. Any position recurring on the search path scores zero, and the search is seeded with the hashes of every played position since the last irreversible move, so the engine avoids repetition when ahead and steers into it when lost — in match play it repeatedly rescued lost games this way. Zero is exact by the antisymmetry of (2). Absorbing states are the boundary condition of the whole thermodynamics: they carry no entropy, feed no thermometer, trip no scheduler, and set no branching — a constitutional rule, because every mechanism that ever let a mate or a repetition act as a thermal energy has failed in play.

8. Quiescence as relaxation; truncation as bath-width

Thermodynamic state variables belong to (locally) equilibrated systems, and a position with hanging pieces is mid-collision. Captures, promotions, and check evasions are relaxed deterministically — alpha-beta at T = 0 with MVV-LVA ordering and delta pruning whose margin includes the optionality swing one capture can cause (~T·Δln W) — and only quiet positions enter an ensemble. This deterministic relaxation is the pre-thermal stage whose size is the Qℓ of (S2); extending what counts as "forcing" is exactly the depth tax (S5).

In the main search, alpha-beta cutoffs are unsound (every child contributes to Z) but contributions decay exponentially: at depth ≥ 2 a shallow ranking pass orders the children, and only those within 3T of the best (capped at Wmax = 16) are fully recursed; the tail keeps its shallow values as its ~1% share of Z. The truncation width is set by the bath — this is precisely (S1): cold searches narrow toward best-line probing where minimax is exact, hot searches keep many live branches. Two children are always fully resolved regardless of temperature — a one-level system yields no fluctuation sample (the thermometer would only confirm itself and freeze), and verifying the runner-up catches a shallow misranking of the best move.

The equilibrium caveat, made explicit (and see §11). "State variables belong to equilibrated systems" is the right statement for a single position measured under its protocol. It is not true of a whole game: measured along the engine's own best play, F carries a persistent negative current — it does not return to itself under deepening as a conserved state function would. Each position is an equilibrium reading; the trajectory through them is a driven steady state. §11 makes this the organizing fact of play.

9. Chemical potential: pieces, and the tempo as the (N+1)-th piece

With pP the Boltzmann weight of all moves originating from piece P, freezing P in place costs free energy μP = −T ln(1 − pP) (5) (the ideal-gas/independent-pieces limit). A piece with μP ≈ 0 is thermodynamically idle whatever its material worth; the piece carrying most of the ensemble weight is what the position is about.

9.1 The tempo

Apply (5) to the tempo — the piece whose moves are all of them — and the formula seems to diverge (p = 1) until the ensemble is extended by the null move as a microstate, with energy Qpass the relaxed value of passing. On Z′ = Z + eQpass/T, deleting the tempo's moves leaves exactly the pass, (5) applies verbatim, and it closes to

μtempo = −T ln π′pass = T · ln(1 + eχ̃/T) ,  χ̃ ≡ F − Qpass (6)

— a softplus, at the bath temperature, of the raw susceptibility reading χ̃. Every limit is chess: when the tempo genuinely matters (χ̃ ≫ T), μtempo → χ̃; in zugzwang (χ̃ < 0) it goes smoothly to zero — the tempo of a player in zugzwang is a worthless particle, no special case needed; the crossover width is T itself. One formula prices rook, queen, pawn, and initiative. The reading is same-parity (a difference of two T = 0 quiescent relaxations one null apart), so the interior entropy ladder never enters and the T·S choice premium is excluded — what remains is the tactical tempo alone, stable under deepening. The gauge zero is χ̃ = 0 ↦ μtempo = T·ln 2, and the trébuchet certification (§12) shows the instrument landing on it exactly.

10. The two-temperature ensemble: prophylaxis and the generalized Gibbs form

King safety and prophylaxis resisted every static formulation we tried: whether a threat is sound is not a counting fact but a search fact, and each attempted leaf term was the standard hand-crafted evaluator rederived in disguise. The model's division of labor points elsewhere: measure it the way physics measures stiffness — perturb and relax. For each candidate move, the tempo-susceptibility probe gives the opponent their reply followed by a free tempo (a null move for the mover) and relaxes through quiescence, in which unsound sacrifices refute themselves. The reading χ̃ = Q − Vtaxed feeds (6), and each candidate acquires a tempo chemical potential.

The opponent is a tempo reservoir: their forcing moves annihilate the mover's tempi, with occupancy w the fraction of quiet quiescence-entry positions whose stand-pat is refuted by a forcing line. The evaluation is the grand potential under tempo exchange:

Φ = F − w · (μtempo − μ̄) (7)

with μ̄ the minimum over candidates (gauge fixing: only μ differences drive exchange). The linear-in-w form is the quenched average over the reservoir's action — the correct choice for an adversary who chooses rather than samples.

10.1 The generalized Gibbs ensemble

Equations (2), (7), and the Gibbs-leaf paradigm are three faces of one object. A system with more than one conserved charge {Ik} does not equilibrate to the ordinary Gibbs state but to the generalized one, ρ ∝ exp(−Σk λk Ik), a separate multiplier for each charge. Chess has two: our optionality and theirs. Our two log-degeneracies Ius = ln Wus, Ithem = ln Wthem are the charges, and the leaf value is their Legendre transform with two multipliers (here the energy is bare material, as in the first term of (2)):

G = material + Tus·ln Wus − Tthem·ln Wthem ,  κ ≡ Tthem / Tus (G)

The single-temperature Helmholtz leaf (2) is the diagonal Tus = Tthem = T; the grand potential (7) is the version in which the them-charge is exchanged with a reservoir at price μtempo; and the Gibbs-leaf paradigm — our activity priced at the zero-point floor Tus = T₀ (degeneracy pressure), theirs at the full bath Tthem = T — is the off-diagonal case κ = T/T₀ that encodes the adversarial asymmetry directly: their optionality is a chooser's, priced high; ours is a committed side's, priced at the quantum floor. This is why an asymmetric leaf can cure a positional blunder (the Alekhine) that the symmetric one cannot — and why it is opponent-dependent in strength (§12). The ratio κ is not free: it is the ratio of the two reservoirs' temperatures, and it is measured (κ̂ = T̂them/Tus ≈ 0.6–0.8 on the certified corpus). Whether a single κ generalizes across positions is open — it overshoots on some and drops below the floor on others — but the form is fixed by the GGE, not fitted.

Where the susceptibility comes from. The two-temperature reading is not decoration: it is already inside the initiative instrument. The Curie–Weiss susceptibility χ = b̂/2(T − T̂c) that amplifies the scheduler's uncertainty (§12) has the gap between the two reservoir temperatures in its denominator — our bath T against the opponent's condensation temperature T̂c — and diverges as T → T̂c. The initiative is the current between the two reservoirs; its response is maximal exactly when their temperatures meet, which is the Curie point the deconfounded cooling scan found adversarial chess to sit at. The GGE is the equilibrium (state-function) side of the same physics whose trajectory side is §11.

11. The game as a non-equilibrium steady state

Every section so far describes a position — an equilibrium reading under a protocol. Play is a trajectory through positions, and the trajectory is not an equilibrium. The state-function residual test asks whether F is a martingale under the engine's own best play — whether, sampled at matched parity two plies apart, it returns to itself in expectation. It does not:

σprod ≡ − ⟨ ΔF ⟩same-parity > 0 (N1)

measured. F carries a persistent negative current: the premium bleeds under deepening (regression slope β ≈ −0.88; only ~12% of the premium survives at depth 3), while the energy part ⟨Q⟩ is very nearly conserved. In the language of irreversible thermodynamics this is entropy production, and the position-level equilibrium of §1–§10 coexists with a game-level steady state that produces it — a non-equilibrium steady state (NESS), stationary in its statistics but sustaining a constant current.

11.1 What the current is: the winner's curse, in closed form

The premium is not only optionality value; it is also selection bias, and the two are provably the same number. Add i.i.d. Gumbel(0, T) noise εa to the child values and take the max: the argmax is distributed exactly as the softmax π (the Gumbel-max identity), and

Eε[ maxa(Qa + εa) ] = F + T·γ  ⟹  Eε[max] − ⟨Q⟩π = T·(S + γ) (N2)

(γ the Euler–Mascheroni constant). The soft backup's value F is, up to the constant offset T·γ, the expected maximum of noisy estimates — and the optimizer's curse of selecting under that noise is exactly T·(S + γ), whose model-carried part is the premium T·S. So the interior premium (2) is simultaneously the value of having options and the systematic optimism of backing up a max over uncertain children. This is the identity behind the program's central empirical law, verified six ladders deep:

The premium meta-law. The interior choice premium at the full bath temperature is load-bearing at exactly its uncorrected size. Deflation by count (mean backup), by structure (basins), by category (king-move exclusion), by measured sibling correlation (the quenched, nested-GEV premium), or by per-option measured resolution (the heteroscedastic σ premium) all reprice the same T·S and all read negative in the gauntlet — and the one setting that cures the model's signature positional blunder is the same setting that collapses its strength. By (N2) this is forced: you cannot subtract the optimism without subtracting the value, because they are one term. The lawful cure cannot subtract what F over-prices; it must add what F cannot see (§11.3).

11.2 The drive: the horizon deficit, and Onsager's form

A NESS has entropy production σprod = Σ Jk Xk — currents times affinities. Here one channel dominates. The affinity is the horizon deficit A(d): the type-b content — specific off-horizon lines — living between the search depth d and infinity, formally A(d) ∝ V*(∞) − F(d), with A(∞) = 0 (infinite depth is equilibrium: F becomes the exact martingale V* and the premium and its bleed both vanish). The current J is the rate at which re-optimization corrects the previously over-priced premium. In linear response J = L·A(d), so

σprod = L · A(d)² . (N3)

This reframes the entire experimental record as one statement: the drive A(d) falls only when the search reaches off-horizon content — by depth generally, or by a forcing extension for the forcing subclass it can resolve — while everything else moves the mobility L or merely reslices the budget of §5. The σeff scheduler's measured +2.5 points (and its halving of mate-losses at equal depth) is exactly a NESS controller — it lowers σprod by placing depth where A is largest. qCheck illustrates the two-sidedness: it lowers A on the tactical subclass it reaches (the king-march nets), but its depth tax (S5) raises A on the positional majority it now searches shallower, and since that majority dominates, the net dissipation rises — the fat tail of which is a catastrophic premium correction: a position read at +6♙ that is in fact being mated, F crashing to −∞ off-horizon. The premium-deflation graveyard is the attempt to cut T·S — the model's estimate of A — without touching A itself; (N3) says that cannot lower the true dissipation, because the estimate is a width-side quantity and the drive is depth-side.

The exposure verdict, in these terms. Twenty-three "complacent-then-mated" positions, recovered objectively from the gauntlets, decide between two worlds: Heval (the refutation is horizon-free structure the ensemble misprices → reprice it) and Hsearch (the refutation is off-horizon → attention only). Stripping both entropy channels at the shallow horizon reveals zero of 23 — the loss is simply not in the shallow tree — so the disease is Hsearch, the affinity A(d), not the pricing. And F's entropy at these positions is a mild penalty (median −1.1♙: the danger sense already firing on the opponent's optionality, ln Wthem rising faster than ln Wus), not the "open-king bonus" once supposed — a bounded ~1♙ signal against a −∞ off-horizon mate. The catastrophe is the horizon, quantitatively.

11.3 The frontier: an affinity you can measure without paying the depth

The scheduler today triggers on σeff = T√(1 + (μ̄χ)²), a width-side quantity — the residual noise of completed measurements, blind by construction to what deeper search has not yet seen (it is evaluated at the current node; A lives past the current horizon). It allocates against the wrong affinity. The lawful move is a controller whose affinity is A(d) — and the objection that "a trajectory blindness has no cheap local detector" (every attempt to find a per-position signature of self-indulgence has failed, because the blindness is a specific line revealed only by searching it) is answered by an established identity:

Harada–Sasa. In a NESS the entropy production equals the integrated violation of the fluctuation–dissipation theorem — the gap between the response function and the correlation function of the observable. Translated: one need not see beyond the horizon to estimate A(d). The same-parity value revision the lab already mines (the d−2→d drift; the per-child TT-history residual σa) is a fluctuation; its systematic part minus its symmetric part is the local dissipation, hence A(d) — provided the NESS is stationary, i.e. the winner's curse autocorrelates along the trajectory. And it does: the ~12% survival at depth 3 (N1) is precisely that persistence. A dissipation-triggered scheduler banks thinking time where recent FDT-violation is largest — where value is still moving with depth — reading the horizon deficit off dissipation it has already paid for, rather than off width-noise.

derived; not yet verified. Two honest caveats gate any implementation, in the order the method requires (derive → check the code computes the derived quantity → only then measure). First, (N3)'s linear response assumes near-equilibrium, and the tail that actually loses games is far from it — whether the FDT-violation estimate reaches the catastrophic tail, or only the bulk, is the open question, and it is the same tactical-9%/positional-91% split the depth-marker study found. Second, the estimator must be shown to compute the Harada–Sasa gap on the quantities the transposition table already stores before a single game is played. The correspondence does not promise the frontier is fertile; it promises this is the one channel not already refuted — an allocator of the conserved ln N (§5) against the measured dissipation (N1), which is the only lever that reads A(d) without spending the depth to see it.

The systematic frame behind this frontier — the graveyard read as a scan of a generalized-Gibbs charge lattice, the constitution recovered as that ensemble's consistency conditions, the effective temperature Teff seated as one of four named-but-empty charge cells, and the pre-registered information-decomposition and two-slope-FDT protocol that seats or refuses each candidate — is worked out in docs/gge_charge_cohort.html (also .pdf).

The information-theoretic restatement. Depth extracts information about the true value: A(d) ∝ H(V* | treed), the residual entropy of the outcome given the depth-d search, falling by ΔI = H(V*|treed) − H(V*|treed+1) per ply. The premium T·S is the engine's local estimate of that residual uncertainty; the type-a/type-b boundary is precisely where the estimate holds (T·S ≈ A, collective content recoverable by a better backup) versus fails (T·S ≪ A, a specific line — at the catastrophes T·S is a bounded penalty while A = ∞). By (S2) each bit of value-information costs nodes at the rate S̄ sets — the same entropy again, now as the price of knowing. A dissipation-triggered controller is an attempt to estimate A from measured ΔI instead of from the mispredicting proxy T·S.

12. The experimental record

The redesign was driven by adversarial testing against a deliberately naive alpha-beta engine whose evaluation is pure classical material (a material-truth oracle that punishes hung pieces and values nothing else) and against the first-edition engine itself. Equal time per move, color-swapped pairs, small opening book. The record below is organized by what each result established about the frame above; full run-by-run data live in tests/results/INDEX.md and the commit history.

  • The physical repairs that made the engine sound. First edition vs the oracle: lost 0–2, with material-losing and material-keeping moves backing up at equal values (the 1:1 mobility-energy rate) and temperatures of 7–9 in collapsing positions (per-node T). The fixes were each physics, not tuning: ensemble weighting of the thermometer (refuted moves are not heat), same-parity sampling (the entropy ladder is not heat — the runaway cured), and the zero-point floor (quiet positions do not freeze into planless repetition). Final model vs the oracle: won the match, +2 −1 =9 — it holds material against a dedicated material engine and converts activity into the winning margin. Vs the first edition: 10–1, fewer than half the material blunders.
  • The initiative has a lawful, measured equation of state. Across ~12,500 candidate readings the two-level law β = tanh(Δμ/2T) captures sign and monotone structure (r = 0.61, every bin correct), steeper than thermal near zero (initiative self-reinforces) and saturating at |β| ≈ 0.6 (alternation forbids full polarization). The Curie–Weiss fit gives self-coupling J = 3.5 Q-units ≈ 1.8♙ (CI 2.0–4.5) and b = 0.63. The cooling scan then found J opponent-dependent — strong against a punishing adversary (criticality ratio 0.79→0.82→1.02 as moves freeze), barely coupled against one's own mirror — so J is a reservoir property, and adversarial chess is played at the initiative's Curie point within error. The structural share Jstructure = T·bite (bite = ⟨ln Wfree − ln Wforced⟩ ≈ 1.8–2.0) is the random-defense reference state; the excess Ĵ − Jstructure is the skill signal, zero-referenced by the rules. This is the reservoir whose temperature is the Tthem of (G) and whose condensation point is the T̂c in χ.
  • The trébuchet certification. An exact KPK-plus-blocked-KPKP tablebase (tests/zugzwang.js) certifies the true mutual full-point zugzwang; there μtempo reads T·ln 2 to displayed precision (χ̃ = 0: no tactical tempo), versus ≈26 Q-units at a hot control. The annealed F reads ≈0 for both colors on a certified loss, because the bath (T ≈ 4.7) exceeds the pawn gap — the ensemble thermally washes the zugzwang out, as a Boltzmann average must when kBT exceeds the level splitting — yet argmax playout converts the win in both directions: selection is a T → 0 quench into a ground state the thermal observable cannot resolve. Zugzwang is a broken-symmetry, frozen-phase object, and the two-temperature reading of §10 is why the tempo's sign is what carries it.
  • Thinking time as latent heat: the σeff scheduler, +2.5 at equal time. The stopping rule σeff = T√(1+(μ̄b̂/2(T−T̂c))²) < ΔQ₁₂ banks frozen decisions' time and draws it back near criticality (up to 4× base). Corrected for the bank-accounting and absorbing-gap bugs, it scored 9/12 vs SF-1500 against the 6.5/12 flux baseline — the strongest result recorded, and (N3)'s NESS controller in practice. It is, however, allocated against a width-side affinity (§11.3): four subsequent criterion modifications (basin-gap freeze 7.5, alloc+schedule 6.5, absorbing-risk guard 6) all read at or below it, and the guard's own replay refuted the "unstable-freeze" hypothesis (d3-confirmed freezes are as unstable as d2 ones) — the clock was never the disease.
  • Kinetics: depth is purchasable, and neutral. Root allocation (alloc) applies the interior truncation law (S1) to the root — the scheduler brought inside the move; frozen children price Z but feed no thermometer sample and need not be argmax. It is strength-neutral (7.5/12 = baseline) with the coolest bath of any config (mean T 8.2 vs 16.6) and thermal runaway nearly halved in play. Basin-hopping truncation, which buys real depth (mean 3.43→4.16), scored lower (6/12): deeper annealing into the same landscape sharpens F's own self-indulgence (more early-queen sorties), it does not heal it. The correspondence (§5) explains both — depth is bought by spending ln N, and by the meta-law the evaluation is the binding constraint, so free depth nets to neutral.
  • Forcing extension: real, and depth-taxed. Check-aware quiescence (qCheck) is a correct tactical instrument — oracle +6 −0 =6, finding Légal's Nxe5 at main-depth 1, and it provably reaches off-horizon king-march mates (reveals 6/10 exposure catastrophes at d2). It is strength-neutral in the gauntlet (≈5.5/12), because by the depth-marker study it harvests only the tactical ~9% of depth-sensitivity while paying the depth tax (S5) on the positional 91%. Its scheduler marriage failed hardest and most instructively (3/12, mate-losses 2→8): the autopsy showed not anti-synergy but the depth tax (S5) — qCheck halves depth in every configuration with no scheduler at all, and the scheduler's edge is depth-conditional, so the two compete for the same ln N. A depth-costly instrument and a depth-conditional one do not compose at fixed budget. This is the composition law of §5.2, learned in play.
  • The value-side dead ends, and why they are one dead end. The leaf tempo charge (refuted twice — one-sided at the horizon, the parity violation of §3); the susceptibility probe as a state energy (1.5/12 — a shallow mate-detector overruling the deep search at weight w ≈ 0.7, the absorbing-state rule violated on the probe's own reading); the six premium-deflation ladders (§11.1). Each is an instance of the constitution's rule 3 — instruments may act as ensemble parameters or attention, never as state energies added to Q — and each confirms the meta-law from a different direction. The asymmetric Gibbs leaf (G) is the one value-side move that adds structure rather than subtracting premium: it cures the Alekhine with the premium intact and reads −4 at SF-1500 but +1 at SF-1600 (a measured, opponent-dependent sign flip; n=12, unresolved) — consistent with the meta-law's corollary that optionality-seeking is insurance whose premium is paid against weak opposition and recovered against strong.

That a sequence of purely physical repairs turned a blundering engine into a sound one, and that the same frame now predicts its own dead ends — the depth tax as a node-budget identity, the premium's immovability as a Gumbel identity — is the encouraging evidence that the thermodynamic frame is doing real work here, rather than decorating a chess engine with vocabulary.

13. The measurement apparatus

The dashboard reports, per position: the material energies Uw, Ub; the measured bath temperature T and root entropy S; ⟨Q⟩, TS, and F; the Schottky readings Ceff = C(Tbath) and C* with the phase label; the move ensemble (Qa, πa); the chemical potentials; the live opponent thermometer Ĵ and the reply-independence instrument λ̂; and time series of Eval, T, S, TS, Ceff, and C* across the game. Four dedicated instruments visualize the physics: the capacity curve C(T) with the peak (C*, T̂) and the bath marker (where it sits is the phase); the annealing trace (bath T per deepening iteration on the zero-point floor — the cooling schedule of §5 made visible); the phase strip under the history graph; and the optional μ overlay on the board (ring intensity ∝ each piece's chemical potential). A Lab panel exposes the test-only hooks by hand; any active field is stamped on the header (⚠ LAB), so a perturbed reading can never be mistaken for honest play.

The headline Eval is everywhere the argmax-Q value in White-POV pawns — the energetic part alone, comparable across engines — while F carries the optionality term on its own row. Stockfish runs in a separate worker as an external reference thermometer and never influences play. There is no temperature knob anywhere: T is a reading, not a setting. The one control that remains — thinking time — is physically meaningful: it is the annealing schedule and, by (S3), the reached depth; a longer think is a colder, deeper engine, at a rate the position's own entropy sets.

14. What this might tell us about chess

  • Depth is a thermodynamic quantity. The reached depth is not a free parameter of the analysis but d = (ln(t/τ₀) − ln Qℓ)/S̄ — the log time budget, net of forcing relaxation, divided by the position's own entropy. Sharp positions (high S) are shallow-searched at fixed time because they are sharp; the same entropy that makes a position rich to value makes it expensive to search. Whether human calculation depth scales inversely with a position's branching entropy the same way is a concrete, measurable question.
  • Play is dissipative, and the dissipation is the winner's curse. F is not conserved along a game; it bleeds a measured current (N1) that is, in closed form, the optimizer's curse of a soft max over uncertain lines (N2). The strength ceiling is a statement about that current: no repricing lowers it (the premium is one dial), only reaching the off-horizon content does. This is a falsifiable claim about where engine — and perhaps human — errors come from: not from mis-valuing what is seen, but from the systematic optimism of choosing among things not yet resolved.
  • The initiative is a near-critical degree of freedom — when someone makes it one. Adversarial chess is played at the initiative's spontaneous-polarization transition within error: small advantages in forcing potential produce disproportionate, self-reinforcing swings. Because J is a reservoir property, the right instrument is a running Ĵ estimated from the game record — an opponent thermometer with no norm of "good play" imported — and the master-games experiment is two questions: what ratio do humans play at, and how fast can Ĵ separate a club player from a grandmaster?
  • C*(position) and κ(position) are parameter-free invariants. The largest evaluation-fluctuation capacity a position can sustain, and the ratio of the two sides' optionality temperatures. Mapping them over master games — openings versus technique, calm versus sharp players — is an experiment this instrument can run today.
  • Optionality has a measured exchange rate. With energy purely material and entropy purely counting, the value of activity in pawns is T·Δln W with T measured — of order half a pawn when uncertain, nearly nothing when resolved. Whether human play implies a similar rate is an open, quantitative question.

Thermodynamic Chess — Project Status

fablethermochess · July 2026 · a lab notebook summary

1 · The goal, and the design philosophy

Build a chess engine that evaluates positions by statistical thermodynamics instead of minimax — a position is an ensemble of continuations, its value is a free energy F = ⟨Q⟩ + T·S — and make the implementation coherent enough that it stops being a metaphor and starts being an instrument: something that can genuinely tell us things about chess (and maybe about thermodynamics) that we didn't put into it.

The constitution. Every quantity in the model must be one of three things: geometry (piece values are mean empty-board reach), counting (entropy is Boltzmann's ln W over move counts), or measurement (temperature is the search's own measured revision noise; every later constant — J, b, κ, T̂c, bite — is measured too). No tuned constants, no imported chess knowledge, no norm of "good play" anywhere.
Rules learned the hard way, now load-bearing: (1) Absorbing states are not thermal — mates and repetitions carry no entropy, feed no thermometer, and trip no scheduler. (2) Instruments may act as ensemble parameters or attention, never as state energies — the temperature and the clock may act; measured fields added to Q have failed every time. (3) Horizon corrections must be side-symmetric — the leaf entropy term lives because it counts both sides; one-sided charges die by parity. (4) Negative results are results — every failure below is committed, with an autopsy.

2 · What we've come up with

The annealed model. Energy = geometric material only. Leaf entropy = T·(ln Wus − ln Wthem). Temperature = measured: the Boltzmann-weighted, same-parity residual of the search's own value revisions, damped, riding a zero-point floor derived from the mobility spectrum. Deepening is cooling; a longer think is a colder engine; there is no temperature knob anywhere in the interface.
Strength. Beats the pure-material oracle (+2−1=9), beats first-edition 10–1, best result 9/12 vs Stockfish-1500 — with zero hand-written chess knowledge. Sits between SF-1500 and SF-1600 at 1s/move. First unprompted castles arrived from search depth alone (including one queenside).
Thinking time as the latent heat of decision. The σeff scheduler stops when value uncertainty falls below the top-two gap and banks the remainder; near-critical decisions draw the bank back out. +1.5 points at equal average time — the largest single strength gain of the project, achieved without touching the evaluation.
The initiative has an equation of state. Who owns the forcing future (β, measured by census) obeys β = tanh(Δμ/2T) sign-perfectly, with two lawful deviations: self-reinforcement (Curie–Weiss J > 0) and alternation saturation (b < 1). J is opponent-dependent (strong vs a punishing adversary, weak vs one's own mirror — established by a three-cell deconfounding design), and its structural share comes from pure counting: forced replies have smaller menus, giving a dimensionless criticality ratio ≈ 0.6–0.75, a constant of chess. Against Stockfish the cold-bulk ratio reaches 0.79 → 0.82 → 1.02: adversarial chess is played at the initiative's Curie point, within error.
Certified instruments. Thermometer invariant under move-enumeration order to 4·10−16 and bitwise-deterministic at fixed depth. A machine-built tablebase certified the true trébuchet; μtempo reads its gauge zero there exactly; zugzwang is a frozen-phase object (invisible in F above the pawn gap, yet argmax play — a T→0 quench — converts the certified win both directions). An opponent thermometer (Ĵ, windowed Curie–Weiss fit with split-half error bars, structural prior) runs live. A reply-partition redundancy meter distinguishes real flexibility from correlated hedges (trap neff = 3.5 vs sound neff = 10.6 at identical nominal entropy).
The laboratory. The dashboard is an instrument panel: capacity curve with phase marker, annealing trace, phase strip, μ board overlay, opponent-Ĵ row, clock row, redundancy row. The test harness carries counterfactual knobs (pin the bath; reprice only the leaf; retemper only the opponent) — external fields for decomposition experiments. Everything reproducible from tests/.

3 · Current problems

Self-indulgence (the Alekhine blunder). 1.e4 Nf6 2.e5 d6?? 3.exf6 — the engine hangs a knight because the interior choice premium T·ln Z pays it for nominal flexibility: thirty replies that are all fine iff the opponent declines one killer move are one hedge counted thirty times. Decomposition (three-knob grid) convicts our own interior ensembles (~3.75 of the swing) — not the leaf (~0.3), not opponent softness (~0.8–1.9). The decision flips at T* ≈ 1.0 — exactly the zero-point floor — so no honest cooling can fix it. This is the central open problem.

Sharpened by the backup-form experiment (July 2026). The premium has an exact identity: under the Gumbel reading of the softmax, F = T·ln Z is what a chooser with value noise T estimates its best option is worth, ⟨Q⟩π is what it achieves, and F − ⟨Q⟩π = T·S — the interior premium is the optimizer's own estimation bias (winner's curse), term for term. Removing it wholesale (mean/max backups, tests/backup_forms.js) cures the blunder at every temperature and never loses to the material oracle — and collapses against Stockfish (3.5 and 5 / 12 vs the 7.5 baseline), mated while ahead in material: the premium is also the menu-collapse gradient, the engine's only interior danger sense. The curse and the danger sense are the same term read from opposite sides. The surviving direction: keep the premium max-anchored and deflate only its correlated share, V = maxQ + λ̂·(T·ln Z − maxQ) with λ̂ the measured independence of sibling revisions (a drift-centered redo of the revision-covariance instrument). Two legs since measured (tests/premT_scan.js, tests/covariance_probe.js): repricing only the premium (the premT hook) crosses at premT ≈ 0.75–1.0 — the zero-point floor again, seen from the premium axis — so no global scale separates blunder-cure from danger sense and the deflation must be per-node; and the λ̂ instrument (v3: drift-centered, reply-grouped) validates on all five predictions (trap 0.60 / sound 0.91 / middlegame 0.92 / Q-en-prise 0.56 / startpos 0.87), ordering correctly exactly where v1 ordered backwards. Next: per-node λ̂ from the shallow-pass→full-pass revisions the truncation already computes, then the usual ladder. The app now carries a Lab panel — the harness hooks, hand-applied; active fields are stamped on every reading.
The effective-entropy premium doesn't yet cure it. Pricing the premium at reply-partition multiplicity (one refutation = one option) is implemented and toggleable in the UI, but on the motivating position both variants slightly worsen the margin — interior reply-sharing is pervasive and the sound line is itself forcing. Instrument validated; mechanism on probation pending the gauntlet.
Thermal runaway in decided positions. The T·ln W feedback can boil the bath (T ~ 10²–10⁴) in won/lost positions. Documented, excluded from fits by declared cuts, uncured.
The SF-1600 cliff. 7–9/12 at Elo 1500 collapses to 1/12 at 1600 (partly Stockfish's nonlinear strength mapping, partly real). Depth is bounded by softmax breadth — full-width Z reaches d2–d4 in middlegames where alpha-beta reaches d8+.
J is hard to pin down. A 64-move window carries ±1.75 of error by information-theoretic necessity; supercritical fits are unidentifiable (the bistable branch fits anything). The estimator is honest about this, but per-game opponent reads stay coarse.
Engine doesn't castle. Prophylactic moves are never made for threats beyond evaluation horizon. We are hoping to find enough information about "good play" baked into the structure of the search tree itself that behaviors like this can made to emerge through improvement of the physical metahpor rather than hardcoded strategic insights.

Thermodynamic Chess — Project Status

fablethermochess · July 2026 · a lab notebook summary

1 · The goal, and the design philosophy

Build a chess engine that evaluates positions by statistical thermodynamics instead of minimax — a position is an ensemble of continuations, its value is a free energy F = ⟨Q⟩ + T·S — and make the implementation coherent enough that it stops being a metaphor and starts being an instrument: something that can genuinely tell us things about chess (and maybe about thermodynamics) that we didn't put into it.

The constitution. Every quantity in the model must be one of three things: geometry (piece values are mean empty-board reach), counting (entropy is Boltzmann's ln W over move counts), or measurement (temperature is the search's own measured revision noise; every later constant — J, b, κ, T̂c, bite — is measured too). No tuned constants, no imported chess knowledge, no norm of "good play" anywhere.
Rules learned the hard way, now load-bearing: (1) Absorbing states are not thermal — mates and repetitions carry no entropy, feed no thermometer, and trip no scheduler. (2) Instruments may act as ensemble parameters or attention, never as state energies — the temperature and the clock may act; measured fields added to Q have failed every time. (3) Horizon corrections must be side-symmetric — the leaf entropy term lives because it counts both sides; one-sided charges die by parity. (4) Negative results are results — every failure below is committed, with an autopsy.

2 · What we've come up with

The annealed model. Energy = geometric material only. Leaf entropy = T·(ln Wus − ln Wthem). Temperature = measured: the Boltzmann-weighted, same-parity residual of the search's own value revisions, damped, riding a zero-point floor derived from the mobility spectrum. Deepening is cooling; a longer think is a colder engine; there is no temperature knob anywhere in the interface.
Strength. Beats the pure-material oracle (+2−1=9), beats first-edition 10–1, best result 9/12 vs Stockfish-1500 — with zero hand-written chess knowledge. Sits between SF-1500 and SF-1600 at 1s/move. First unprompted castles arrived from search depth alone (including one queenside).
Thinking time as the latent heat of decision. The σeff scheduler stops when value uncertainty falls below the top-two gap and banks the remainder; near-critical decisions draw the bank back out. +1.5 points at equal average time — the largest single strength gain of the project, achieved without touching the evaluation.
The initiative has an equation of state. Who owns the forcing future (β, measured by census) obeys β = tanh(Δμ/2T) sign-perfectly, with two lawful deviations: self-reinforcement (Curie–Weiss J > 0) and alternation saturation (b < 1). J is opponent-dependent (strong vs a punishing adversary, weak vs one's own mirror — established by a three-cell deconfounding design), and its structural share comes from pure counting: forced replies have smaller menus, giving a dimensionless criticality ratio ≈ 0.6–0.75, a constant of chess. Against Stockfish the cold-bulk ratio reaches 0.79 → 0.82 → 1.02: adversarial chess is played at the initiative's Curie point, within error.
Certified instruments. Thermometer invariant under move-enumeration order to 4·10−16 and bitwise-deterministic at fixed depth. A machine-built tablebase certified the true trébuchet; μtempo reads its gauge zero there exactly; zugzwang is a frozen-phase object (invisible in F above the pawn gap, yet argmax play — a T→0 quench — converts the certified win both directions). An opponent thermometer (Ĵ, windowed Curie–Weiss fit with split-half error bars, structural prior) runs live. A reply-partition redundancy meter distinguishes real flexibility from correlated hedges (trap neff = 3.5 vs sound neff = 10.6 at identical nominal entropy).
The laboratory. The dashboard is an instrument panel: capacity curve with phase marker, annealing trace, phase strip, μ board overlay, opponent-Ĵ row, clock row, redundancy row. The test harness carries counterfactual knobs (pin the bath; reprice only the leaf; retemper only the opponent) — external fields for decomposition experiments. Everything reproducible from tests/.
Check-aware quiescence: the engine finds sacrifices (July 2026). Quiescence is the model's T = 0 relaxation of the fast forcing degrees of freedom before the slow ones are sampled thermally — and it resolved captures but not checks. A check is equally a fast forcing move (its reply menu genuinely collapses — real constraint bite, unlike a queen sortie's fake forcing), so it belongs in the pre-thermal relaxation with captures; its omission left check-based tactics invisible at every reachable depth (the Légal sacrifice read −14 and stalled at depth 3 even at 20 s, because the mating move Nd5# is a non-capture check that capture-only quiescence stands pat in front of). qCheck adds non-capture checks to the top quiescence plies — a search extension (attention), never a term in Q, symmetric across colors. Oracle +6−0=6 (the strongest material-soundness result of the project — a tactic-finder converts forcing activity into material), and the first mechanism ever to find a sacrifice (Légal's Nxe5, resolved at main-search depth 1). Gauntlet 7/12 vs the 7.5 baseline — neutral, but tied at HALF the main-search depth (mean 1.67 vs 3.43): quiescence tactical resolution substitutes for depth. The only cure that is baseline-neutral without paying the −3.5 Alekhine tax. It cures the tactical horizon only (not the sortie's positional one — correct division of labor); its ceiling is implementation (per-leaf check-detection costs ~1.8 plies), and the fix — a dedicated check-move generator — is the recorded highest-upside next step.
The Gibbs leaf breaks the SF-1600 cliff (July 2026). The owner's paradigm shift: G = U + P·V − T·Sthem — opponent optionality alone pays the measured bath price (their freedom is our forecast noise: the danger sense and the cornering gradient own the whole T dial), while our activity is priced as zero-point pressure work, P = T₀, V = lnWus — degeneracy pressure, the drive that survives convergence, derived from geometry (rule-3 clean; instruments-as-energies died thrice). Roles root-anchored (parity-safe); Gibbs ≡ Helmholtz exactly at T = T₀; the interior premium untouched. Cures the Alekhine at d4 with the premium intact (unique among cures), converts fine70 faster (45 plies), castles both colors at 1600. CORRECTION (same session, comparator hygiene): the initial reading — 4.5/12 against the recorded 1600 baseline of 1/12 — used a stale comparator; a same-harness rebaseline puts the current engine at 3.5/12, so the honest Gibbs-at-1600 result is +1.0 at n=12 — suggestive, unresolved. What survives: the sign flip (Gibbs −4 at 1500, +1 at 1600; sigma negative at both — the 1600 behavior is Gibbs-STRUCTURE-specific, premium intact + opponent-dominant leaf), mate losses 10→6, bath halved, and the first-ever Ĵ-at-Elo corpus (T̂c > 0 on 45% of moves at 1600 vs 12–39% at 1500 — the opponent thermometer discriminates, weakly). At SF-1500 the mechanism reads 3.5/12 (autopsied: early-queen rate UP — the indulgence lives in the untouched interior premium — and the asymmetric leaf is unbounded at runaway T). Optionality pricing looks opponent-dependent, as the measured J already suggested, but the evidence is now honestly graded: an n=24 rebaseline at 1600 or stronger opposition is the recorded next measurement before any Ĵ-gated paradigm selector is built.
Root allocation (the scheduler brought inside the move). The root was the one node exempt from its own truncation law — all n children re-searched full-width every iteration while every interior node keeps only its dominant set. The alloc protocol applies the interior law to the root (3T window re-cut per iteration, top two always live, TRUNC_MAX cap); frozen children keep their standing value in Z, feed no thermometer sample (freshness-parity guard), and may not be argmax (selection stays inside the re-measured set — without that rule the played move can be a stale needle, measured and repaired). Verdict: strength-neutral kinetics — 7.5/12 vs the 7.5 fixed-time baseline with a d8–d9 band appearing in play, the coolest bath of any config (mean T 8.2 vs 16.6), thermal-runaway share nearly halved in play (2.8% vs 4.1% of moves at T>20; runaway clock-stops 13 vs 30) — deeper quenching of decided positions mitigates an open problem. Depth is now purchasable at no strength cost; what depth buys is bounded by the evaluation (below).

3 · Current problems

Self-indulgence (the Alekhine blunder). 1.e4 Nf6 2.e5 d6?? 3.exf6 — the engine hangs a knight because the interior choice premium T·ln Z pays it for nominal flexibility: thirty replies that are all fine iff the opponent declines one killer move are one hedge counted thirty times. Decomposition (three-knob grid) convicts our own interior ensembles (~3.75 of the swing) — not the leaf (~0.3), not opponent softness (~0.8–1.9). The decision flips at T* ≈ 1.0 — exactly the zero-point floor — so no honest cooling can fix it. This is the central open problem.

Sharpened by the backup-form experiment (July 2026). The premium has an exact identity: under the Gumbel reading of the softmax, F = T·ln Z is what a chooser with value noise T estimates its best option is worth, ⟨Q⟩π is what it achieves, and F − ⟨Q⟩π = T·S — the interior premium is the optimizer's own estimation bias (winner's curse), term for term. Removing it wholesale (mean/max backups, tests/backup_forms.js) cures the blunder at every temperature and never loses to the material oracle — and collapses against Stockfish (3.5 and 5 / 12 vs the 7.5 baseline), mated while ahead in material: the premium is also the menu-collapse gradient, the engine's only interior danger sense. The curse and the danger sense are the same term read from opposite sides. The surviving direction: keep the premium max-anchored and deflate only its correlated share, V = maxQ + λ̂·(T·ln Z − maxQ) with λ̂ the measured independence of sibling revisions (a drift-centered redo of the revision-covariance instrument). Two legs since measured (tests/premT_scan.js, tests/covariance_probe.js): repricing only the premium (the premT hook) crosses at premT ≈ 0.75–1.0 — the zero-point floor again, seen from the premium axis — so no global scale separates blunder-cure from danger sense and the deflation must be per-node; and the λ̂ instrument (v3: drift-centered, reply-grouped) validates on all five predictions (trap 0.60 / sound 0.91 / middlegame 0.92 / Q-en-prise 0.56 / startpos 0.87), ordering correctly exactly where v1 ordered backwards. The basin program then built the per-node version: continuations grouped into strategic cohorts by inherent structure (value cohorts of diameter ≤ T × census material flow × initiative regime — validated: the trap line resolves to {dxe5, Ng4} vs 27 knight-losers in two basins), and the interior basin premium counts plans, not moves, in every Z. Its ladder: cures the blunder at every T, keeps the trébuchet visible, tames runaway (bath 8.8 vs 16.6 in play), keeps oracle conversion (+2−1=9), cuts mated-while-ahead losses to one — and still scores 6/12 vs the 7.5 baseline. The four-point gauntlet ladder now reads 7.5 (full premium) > 6 (plans) > 5 (max) > 3.5 (mean): strength orders by premium size, so either the hedge share of the premium is far smaller in real play than the Alekhine case suggests, or 12-game gauntlets cannot resolve 1.5 points — both possibilities stand recorded. The app now carries a Lab panel — the harness hooks, hand-applied; active fields are stamped on every reading.
The effective-entropy premium doesn't yet cure it. Pricing the premium at reply-partition multiplicity (one refutation = one option) is implemented and toggleable in the UI, but on the motivating position both variants slightly worsen the margin — interior reply-sharing is pervasive and the sound line is itself forcing. Instrument validated; mechanism on probation pending the gauntlet.
Thermal runaway in decided positions. The T·ln W feedback can boil the bath (T ~ 10²–10⁴) in won/lost positions. Documented, excluded from fits by declared cuts, uncured.
The SF-1600 cliff — no longer a depth problem. 7–9/12 at Elo 1500 collapses to 1/12 at 1600 (partly Stockfish's nonlinear strength mapping, partly real). The kinetics hypothesis ("depth is bounded by softmax breadth") has now been tested: basin-hop v2 + root allocation bought mean play depth 3.43 → 4.16 with a d6–d9 band (123 moves vs 26) at the same 1 s — and scored lower (6/12), while depth-neutral alloc scored equal. Deeper annealing into the same landscape optimizes F's own flaws more sharply (the hopalloc run played more early queen moves, 2.83/game vs 2.25 — the self-indulgence premium, surfaced by depth); it does not heal them. The binding constraint is the evaluation, not the search kinetics.
The scheduler freezes shallowest exactly where attacks build — and the guard that should fix it didn't, for a reason that matters. 80% of σ_eff freezes fire at depth 2; a forcing capture sequence makes the top-two gap huge while the mating net beyond d2 is invisible to σ_eff (documented: allocsched QGD-W, frozen at d2 through Qxg7/Qxh8/Qxg8, mated at +590). The absorbing-risk guard was then built and tested (pre-registered, tests/freeze_guard_replay.js): deny d2 freezes whose chosen future reads adverse beyond counting error or unmeasured; defer to d3, where a parity-confirmed revision exists. The clock mechanics worked exactly as designed (d2 freezes 115→2, deferrals nearly free) — and the gauntlet read 6/12 vs 9, with the hypothesis refuted by its own metric: d3-confirmed freezes are exactly as unstable as raw d2 ones (32% vs 29% eval-drop rate), and the catastrophe class recurred on full-deadline moves (English-W mated at +1010 after marching its king Kb4→Kd7 at evals +10…+13). The clock was never the disease: every scheduler pathology traced here is downstream of the evaluation. Scheduler ladder: 9 (σ_eff as-is) > 7.5 > 6.5 ≈ 6 — four criterion modifications in a row read negative at n=12. Either σ_eff-as-is is a sharp local optimum, or the single 9/12 run was a favorable draw; an n=24 schedule rebaseline would settle which, cheaply. (The gloss "F prices an open king as an entropy bonus" was the working reading here; the discriminator below now corrects it — at the actual catastrophe positions F's entropy is a net penalty, and the disease is the horizon, not the entropy pricing.)
The exposure discriminator: the catastrophe is the horizon, not the entropy pricing (July 2026). Before building the next evaluation cure, one measurement the program had never made: of the two worlds every dead-end is consistent with — H_eval (the refutation is horizon-free structure the ensemble misprices → reprice it, the Gibbs/asymmetric-reservoir road) versus H_search (the refutation is off-horizon → attention only) — which one are we in? exposure_probe.js settles it on 23 "complacent-then-mated" positions recovered objectively from the recorded gauntlets (engine read +3…+15♙ then got checkmated; config-diverse, so the disease is base-F). Pre-registered rule, certified hooks only (fixed-depth, backup:'max', leafT). Verdict H_search, decisively. The ensemble arm — strip BOTH entropy channels at the shallow horizon — reads cold_local = 0/23: not one catastrophe becomes visible, because the loss is not in the shallow tree at all. No ensemble re-derivation (Gibbs, basin, sigma, exK, or any future one) can see it. Framing correction: the entropy inflation eF−eCold is negative in all 23 (median −1.1♙) — F's entropy is a mild penalty, the danger sense already firing on the opponent's optionality; it fires, but it is a bounded ~1♙ signal against a −∞ off-horizon mate while raw material reads +6…+15 (the engine up a rook/queen and being mated). The "open-king bonus" intuition saw lnWus rise with escape squares and missed that lnWthem rises faster. Median reveal Δ = 7 ply, 15/23 unrevealed even at d8; king-marches sit shallower (Δ≈3.5, forcing-reachable) than material grabs (Δ 7, past d8). Redirect: the lawful cure is neither repricing (Pathway 1 refuted here) nor width allocation (alloc neutral; the guard's 1-ply defer already failed — the nets are 4–8+ ply out) but forcing-line (check) extension to resolution — the qCheck family, the one instrument that provably reaches off-horizon mates (Légal) and already cut mate-losses 10→6 — concentrated on the king-march class the probe shows is within ~4 ply. The flat-to-d8 material-up cases are the honest residue: no search from a single position reaches them.
J is hard to pin down. A 64-move window carries ±1.75 of error by information-theoretic necessity; supercritical fits are unidentifiable (the bistable branch fits anything). The estimator is honest about this, but per-game opponent reads stay coarse.
Engine doesn't castle. Prophylactic moves are never made for threats beyond evaluation horizon. We are hoping to find enough information about "good play" baked into the structure of the search tree itself that behaviors like this can made to emerge through improvement of the physical metahpor rather than hardcoded strategic insights.
